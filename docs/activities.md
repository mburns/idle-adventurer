🎮 Idle Activities Available by Category
Based on the game's enhanced activities system, here are all the available idle activities organized by ability score and type:

💪 Strength Activities

- Athletics Training - Practice climbing, jumping, and swimming (10% daily progress, 5 sp/day)
- Physical Labor - Construction, mining, or heavy lifting work (15% daily progress, earns 2 gp/day)
- Combat Training - Weapon practice and sparring (8% daily progress, 1 gp/day)
- Blacksmithing - Craft weapons and armor (5% daily progress, earns 5 gp/day)

🏃 Dexterity Activities

- Acrobatics Practice - Balance, tumbling, and flexibility training (12% daily progress, 3 sp/day)
- Sleight of Hand Practice - Pickpocketing and lockpicking training (10% daily progress, 2 sp/day)
- Stealth Training - Hiding, sneaking, and camouflage practice (8% daily progress, 1 sp/day)
- Jewelry Making - Craft delicate jewelry and precision items (6% daily progress, earns 8 gp/day)
  Performance Arts - Dancing, juggling, and entertainment (10% daily progress, earns 3 gp/day)

🧠 Intelligence Activities

- Arcana Research - Study magic, spells, and magical theory (7% daily progress, 1 gp/day)
- History Study - Research past events and ancient civilizations (8% daily progress, 5 sp/day)
- Investigation Work - Detective work and puzzle solving (9% daily progress, 3 sp/day)
- Nature Study - Study plants, animals, and natural phenomena (10% daily progress, 2 sp/day)
- Religion Study - Study theology and divine lore (8% daily progress, 4 sp/day)
- Academic Work - Teaching, writing, and research (6% daily progress, earns 4 gp/day)
- Alchemy - Brew potions and study magical substances (5% daily progress, earns potions)

🧘 Wisdom Activities

- Animal Handling - Train animals and practice veterinary skills (10% daily progress, 3 sp/day)
- Insight Practice - People watching and psychology study (8% daily progress, 1 sp/day)
- Medicine Practice - Healing practice and medical research (9% daily progress, 5 sp/day)
- Perception Training - Awareness exercises and sensory training (11% daily progress, 2 sp/day)
- Survival Skills - Foraging, tracking, and wilderness survival (10% daily progress, earns materials)
- Spiritual Practices - Meditation and religious devotion (6% daily progress, free)

🗣️ Charisma Activities
Performance - Music, acting, storytelling, and entertainment (10% daily progress, earns 4 gp/day)

- Social Networking - Building relationships and reputation (7% daily progress, 1 gp/day)

🏋️ Constitution Activities

Fasting & Meditation - Spiritual endurance and self-discipline (8% daily progress, free)

🌍 General Activities
Travel - Explore new locations and discover places (15% daily progress, 1 gp/day)
Faction Work - Complete faction missions and build reputation (10% daily progress, earns 3 gp/day)
Social Events - Attend parties and make connections (8% daily progress, 2 gp/day)
Rest & Recovery - Healing, relaxation, and mental health (20% daily progress, 5 sp/day)
Shopping - Equipment acquisition and market research (10% daily progress, costs 5 gp/day)
Gambling - Games of chance and risk-taking (5% daily progress, variable gold)
Religious Services - Attend ceremonies and spiritual growth (6% daily progress, 1 sp/day)

📊 Activity Mechanics
Progress Scaling: Activities scale with your ability scores (10% bonus per point above 10)
Offline Progress: Activities continue running when you're away from the game
Requirements: Each activity has ability score, gold, and tool requirements
Rewards: Experience points, gold, skills, and special items
Daily Costs: Most activities have ongoing costs that scale with time
The system is designed to provide meaningful progression across all D&D ability scores while maintaining the idle game mechanics of continuous advancement!
